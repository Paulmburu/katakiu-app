QR Component Engineering Rules:
  • Contrast: Pure black/white sharp square modules with standard 4-module quiet zone
  • Container: Exactly 8 px outer container corner radius
  • Integrity: Never place brand logos or text inside the scannable matrix
  • Style: No gradients, circular dots, 3D perspective or barcode hybrid styles
  • Supporting Pass Metadata: Customer name, member ID, verified points balance, countdown timer, merchant instruction
  • Visual note: Design system QR is an illustrative placeholder token and not claimed as live scannable
20. Accessibility & Interaction
  • Minimum Touch Targets: All interactive controls must reserve at least 48 dp hit areas
  • Contrast Compliance: WCAG AA contrast for text against backgrounds (Midnight #071724 on White #FFFFFF ratio > 17:1)
  • Dual Encoding: Status is never conveyed through color alone; always pair text label with icon
  • No Hidden Gestures: Critical flows must be achievable through visible tapped actions
  • Form Proximity: Error messages stay immediately below the responsible input field
21. Kenyan Content & Tone
  • Tone: Clear, respectful, friendly, brief, and responsible
  • Realistic Kenyan Copy: Use genuine names (Amina, Kevin) and merchants (Naivas, Carrefour, Westlands, Market Square)
  • Currency & Values: KES 500 • 1,840 PTS • +254 712 345 678
  • Standard Language: “Scan to earn”, “Purchase confirmed”, “Points pending confirmation”, “Enjoy responsibly”
  • Responsible Conduct: Strictly no alcohol glamour, no consumption targets, no drinking leaderboards, no urgency tactics
22. System Boundary & Do-Not-Use Rules Strict design principles to avoid AI-generated and toy-like aesthetics

  • Fixed 8 px corner radius on cards, buttons, inputs, dialogs
  • Fixed 12 px top radius on bottom sheets
  • Fixed 6 px radius on status chips
  • Thin 1 px borders with #E6EAED

cancel Explicitly Prohibited Anti-Patterns
  • No gradients, glassmorphism, or neon glow effects
  • No giant display typography or bloated button labels
  • No oversized rounded cards, pill buttons, or decorative blobs
  • No logos inside QR codes or distorted QR modules

  • No alcohol consumption gamification or drinking leaderboards
  • No heavy drop shadows or floating multi-layered cards
  • No customer/merchant application screens created on this board
